# R&D: Premium 3D Web Design & AI Agency Best Practices (2026)

## 🌐 Modern Frameworks & Trends
- **Next.js 16+**: High-performance SSR with Turbopack for near-instant interaction.
- **Lenis v1.2+**: Momentum-based smooth scrolling has become the standard for luxury boutiques.
- **Framer Motion 11+**: Orchestrating complex 3D layouts and micro-interactions.
- **Glassmorphism 2.0**: Moving away from flat transparency to "Frosted Obsidian" with refractive light-bending.

## 📈 Elite UI/UX Strategies
1. **Grainy Texture Overlays**: Essential for creating a "tactile" and "cinematic" feel on digital surfaces.
2. **Gold/Emerald Palette Harmony**: Using HSL-tailored colors (e.g., specific Oryn emerald at 135deg gradient).
3. **Reactive Custom Cursors**: Liquid cursors that behave like magnetized particles around clinical elements.
4. **Before/After Elasticity**: Using "friction-based" physics for transformation sliders to simulate physical weight.
